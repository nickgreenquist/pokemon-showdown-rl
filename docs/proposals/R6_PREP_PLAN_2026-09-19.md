## 0. The read that shapes this plan — the R5 loss autopsy (run 2026-09-19)

All 200 R5 replays (`results/ladder/replays_r5`), both sides on the same parser, outcome
agreeing with `R5E.battles.jsonl` on 200/200. "Net hax" = luck events that hit the
opponent minus luck events that hit us (crits taken, freezes taken, full-para / sleep /
frozen turns, misses, confusion self-hits). Played-out battles only (150 of 200; the
other 50 are forfeits, no-shows and timeouts).

| net hax (+ = luck favoured us) | n | our win rate |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ −3 | 52 | **0.19** |
| −2 … −1 | 20 | 0.40 |
| 0 | 12 | 0.67 |
| +1 … +2 | 26 | 0.73 |
| ≥ +3 | 40 | **0.83** |

- Mean net hax **+2.4 in wins, −2.8 in losses**. Losses with even-or-better luck
  ("unforced"): **18 of 72 (25%)**. Wins with even-or-worse luck ("earned"): 26 of 78.
- **By opponent band, played out:** <1200 0.77 (n=22), 1200–1299 0.54 (35), 1300–1399
  0.49 (55), **≥1400 0.35 (34)**. Of the 22 played-out losses to ≥1400, 14 had luck
  against us and 8 did not.
- **Margin of losses:** opponent had 1 mon left in 19, 2 in 26, 3+ in 27. Crushed losses
  (3+ left) carry the worst luck (mean net −2.7 to −9.5).
- **Style parity with the human field, including the ≥1400 slice:** switch share 0.259
  vs 0.287, status-move share 0.216 vs 0.242, Explosion 0.9 vs 1.1 per 100 moves,
  and over all 200 battles zero-damage moves 0.9% vs 1.0% and **Hyper Beam KO rate 0.48
  vs 0.50** with the same recharge-turn count (20 vs 22). No coarse tactical hole is
  visible on either side.

**What it means.** The format is luck-dominated (matches §27's 64% irreducible), our play
edge over the ~1300 pool is real but modest, and the deficit against ≥1400 is not a
style defect the coarse audit can see. Climbing is a **decision-quality** problem in
close games: the value function's ranking (§27.1) and the policy head's margin
(`action_gap.py`, CLEANUP L9) are the instruments that speak to it, and no post-hoc
policy-form change will. This closes "find the tactical hole" as a cheap route and
points the fleet at the critic's signal and the optimizer's noise.

## 2. The R6 fleet (the deliverable) — 6 lanes × 200M, ~50 h, k = 8, anneal = horizon

**Base on every lane:** the credited W recipe (`l2_init_decay 0.02`, critic
`value_sizes [1024,1024]`) **plus the C6 encoder fix in its constant-OBS_DIM form**
(IDEAS 4.6 form (a): effective power in the base-power slot for Seismic Toss / Night
Shade, a flag for Super Fang / Counter; fingerprinted, tape-hash gate re-pinned).
C6 is common-mode across arms and **uncredited by design**; it is measured as a
mechanism (Seismic Toss / Super Fang usage on guaranteed holders, replay-read) and it
makes R5's finals a *different object* inside any mixed committee, which the read
measures rather than assumes.

**Trio A — outcome decomposition on the critic (new IDEAS row 4.11).** KataGo's
1.65× lever, self-play-legal: auxiliary value heads regressing (i) our survivors,
(ii) their survivors, (iii) terminal HP margin, all from the episode's own terminal
observation; critic side only, coefficient ~0.1, `aux_outcome/*` losses and per-target
EV logged from PPO's update. **Mechanism co-primary:** by-turn r² against the rollout
oracle (§27's instrument, `scripts/critic_calibration.py`) on the finals — the lever
must lift the turn-2–8 bucket; critic first-layer srank99 as the second read.

**Trio B — the batch/epochs dose (new IDEAS row 4.12).** `rollout_steps 15360` (batch
122,880), `epochs 2`, `minibatches 120` (1024-row minibatches), LR raised √2× to
3.5e-4 as the starting point; the 12M screen below sets it. Why: the batch lever is the
only lever family here with a measured monotone credit (R2, +0.137 off FP@20) and
nothing above 30,720 has ever run; mid-run `approx_kl` 0.043 at `clip_frac` 0.225
says the surrogate is saturated on noisy advantages; bigger minibatches are faster on
CPU where the update is 65% of wall. **Mechanism co-primary:** per-update `approx_kl`,
`clip_frac`, entropy and EV trajectories at matched steps; win rate secondary.

**Floor and object rule:** the three R5 W finals re-drawn **in the same session** off
FP@20 (never the banked 0.5987); committees ENS3-A, ENS3-B, ENS6, ENS9 (with R5's
finals); the ladder object is the best committee off FP@20 under a pre-stated tie band;
vs SH and the BC-clone leg travel as the anchor battery. Worst case, both levers null:
six more W-recipe members, which is the one known-positive lever (4.8).

**Expected value, stated plainly:** +0.02 to +0.05 off FP@20 on the object; tens of
Glicko, not a hundred. The board's p90 (GXE ~82) needs R7 as well.
