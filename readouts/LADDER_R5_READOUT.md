**Read the opponent-rating columns before the win-rate columns.**
Rematches are rating-matched by construction — opponents met twice skew
stronger, and the opponent-Elo columns above are where you check that —
so the two cells are NOT like-for-like, and a lower rematch win rate is
predicted by the confound alone, with zero memorisation.
**This run came out the OTHER way:** rematch 0.653 (n=98) vs first encounter 0.627 (n=102),
i.e. +0.026 AGAINST the stronger pool (opp Elo mean 1327 vs 1282). The confound
predicts the opposite sign, so it cannot explain this cell
away — and the cell is nowhere near powered to establish the
reverse either. Do not read it as opponents failing to adapt,
and do not read it as us adapting.
This cell is descriptive and attaches to no lever.

## Appendix — opponent-pool concentration (CLEANUP item L1)

Added by hand 2026-09-16 after the maintainer observed mid-run that repeat
opponents might be learning the bot. The generator's obligation (ii) reports the
rematch CELL; this appendix reports the POOL, which is the part that bears on how
many independent draws 200 battles actually buy.

| | |
|---|---|
| rated battles | 200 |
| distinct opponents | 102 |
| opponents met once | 73 |
| battles against a repeat opponent | 127 (**0.635**) |
| games supplied by the top five opponents | 69 (**0.345**) |

Top five: JodiTh 23, NidoRey 14, iranteamo 13, CrangleMcDangle 10, aluminum_boat 9.

**Two adaptation tests, both pre-stated in shape and both NULL:**

| test | early | late | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| repeat opponents (>=4 games), their first halves vs second halves | 0.629 (n=35) | 0.632 (n=38) | +0.003 at 0.03 se |
| first meetings vs all rematches | 0.627 (n=102) | 0.653 (n=98) | +0.026 at 0.38 se |
| first 50 battles vs last 50 battles | 0.580 | 0.600 | +0.020 |

Both deltas run in OUR favour and neither is close to significance, so this run
shows **no evidence that opponents adapted to the object**. It equally shows no
evidence they did not: at these cell sizes nothing under about twenty points of
win rate is resolvable, and the rematch cell additionally faces the
rating-matching confound named in obligation (ii).

**What this does bear on:** Glicko treats 200 battles as 200 independent games,
and 63.5% of them came from opponents already faced, with a third of the run
supplied by five accounts. The rating is the server's and stands as the server
computed it; the EFFECTIVE sample behind it is smaller than n suggests, and that
is a property of an off-peak gen-1 random-battle pool, not of this object.
Recorded as **CLEANUP item L1** with a proposal to replace "ONE CONTINUOUS RUN"
with a pre-registered SPLIT SCHEDULE (sessions across different hours and days,
with its own stopping rule) at the R6 pre-reg. **Not applied to R5**: changing a
schedule mid-run is an unregistered deviation. Descriptive; attaches to no lever.
