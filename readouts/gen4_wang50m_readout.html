What failed, and two defects found while verifying

Part of the result, not footnotes.

Attempt 1 was ops-killed after 13 minutes

Gen-4 pool-seat mask desyncs — U-turn, Baton Pass and choice-lock traffic move the request under the pool seat — tripped a gen-1 recovery cap. Fixed so the pool player re-decides once on the moved request, verified by a 60k-step smoke, and attempt 2 then carried zero surviving desyncs to 50M on all three lanes. A second incident killed one lane on the in-loop eval’s all-or-nothing outcome guard. Both share a shape worth keeping: gen-4 traffic breaks gen-1 guards, and a three-update smoke cannot show it.

R0-5’s post-hoc read is vacuous

It measures the first rung against the last launch line — which after the rollover is a resume — so it prints negative minutes. The gate was genuinely satisfied at launch, not by that read.

The completion auto-chain would have deadlocked on itself

The watcher’s own command line contains the strings python -m rl.train and gen4_wang50m, so both its own guard and the schedule’s refusal check matched the watcher. The schedule was launched by hand after confirming no interpreter process remained and all three finals were on disk. Anchor such a guard on the interpreter path, never on a bare module name a watcher may also mention.

What this does not establish

  • No lever is credited. This is a baseline; no credit line is applied to any number in it.
  • vs-SH is never a ladder number, in either direction. The gen-4 ladder is banked and unrun — the chapter closes on the offline comparison.
  • The gen-4 vs-SH scale is not the gen-1 scale. 0.8788 may not be set beside the gen-1 table’s 0.7959: SH’s competence relative to a learned policy differs by generation.
  • pool_size: 1 was Wang-match fidelity only, and is not evidence about league play either way.
